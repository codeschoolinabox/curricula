level descriptions
  - solo
    - for evaluating responses rather than generating questions
      can see it's shadow in block model's skeleton
    1: pre-structural
    2: uni-structural

    4: relational

  - block model
    - duality: dimensions x levels
    dimensions
      1: text surface - "syntax"/"structure"
        can be answered with ?highlight/?variables/monaco search
      2: execution - "semantics"
        can be answered with [trace]
      3: goals - "purpose"
        can not be answered by determinate analysis
    levels
      1: atoms
        surface - language elements
        execution - operation of a statement
        goals - purpose in context (of the syntax)
      2: blocks
        surface - syntactically or semantically a unit
        execution - operation of a block (sequence of statements)
        goals - sub-goal
      3: relations
        surface - references between blocks
        execution - flowchart (?), (replace "method call" with "statement/operation")
        goals - behavior is built of sub-goals
      4: structure
        surface - overall structure of text
        execution - the "algorithm" (strategy)
        goals - whole program's purpose in context

--- poetry analysis ---
- motivation:
  code may be more like poetry than prose
    much is left unsaid
    much is left to be interpreted by the audience (reader or machine)
    allusions, metaphors and stand-ins are common and central
    often short and concise
    language use may be different from standard spoken/written language
    shape and layout on the page are meaningful (structure, meaning. form, content. especially in python)
  so how are people teaching/learning to analyze poetry?
