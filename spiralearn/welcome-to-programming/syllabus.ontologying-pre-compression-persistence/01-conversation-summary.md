## Round 7 — V/F failure-mode refinement + thinking-vs-process distinction

Substantial refinement to the V/F characterization.

### Key claims locked in R7

- **Twinning is what makes the bridging activity _thinking_ rather than mere
  process.** Sharpens §2 bridging-activities definitions. Design _thinking_
  requires twinning the user; without it, what's happening is _design process_,
  not design _thinking_. Same on F's side.
- **V failure = design process without design thinking** (cargo-culting
  wireframes, personas, A/B tests; symptoms: cannot answer user questions,
  cannot connect features to user needs, cannot provide validations).
- **F failure = computational process without computational thinking**
  (cargo-culting unit tests, patterns, refactoring; symptoms: only black-box not
  glass-box tests; patterns subtly incompatible with language/framework).
- **Twin-failure categories** (ignored / wrong / not-yet-known) STAY and apply
  equally to V and F.
- **The twin / process 2×2** with four corners: pure process / V (Ch3) / F (Ch2)
  / both-twins-state (Ch4 + Ch5).
- **Vibe coding placement**: not bound to one corner; depends on which twin the
  practitioner is holding (or not).
- **Twin-less processes have value** but are not a substitute for twinning. Can
  sometimes reach similar outcomes to twin-without- process in well-bounded
  settings; NOT claimed they outperform.
- **Strict process WITH strong twinning is itself a failure mode**; processes
  are descriptive (music-theory analogy, in passing only).
- **Documentation is a both-hats case**: needs both dev/reader twinning AND
  NM-twinning at the documented software's NM-level. Building an API/library is
  creating a new NM layer.
- **Symptoms ≠ failure modes**: ceremony-without-twin and
  design-thinking-process-without-user-twin are what failures look like from
  outside; the failure is upstream in the twin (or in
  strict-process-enforcement-when-twinning).
