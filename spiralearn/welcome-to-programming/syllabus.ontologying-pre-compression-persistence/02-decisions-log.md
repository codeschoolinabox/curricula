## R7 — V/F failure-mode refinement + thinking-vs-process distinction

Substantial refinement to the V/F characterization in `syllabus.ontology.md` §2
and §3.

| Decision                                                                             | Notes                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Twinning is what makes the bridging activity _thinking_ rather than mere process** | Added to §2 as a sharpening of the bridging-activities definitions. Design _thinking_ requires twinning the user; without that, what's happening is _design process_ (wireframes, personas, etc.) not design _thinking_. Same on F's side: _computational thinking_ requires NM-twinning; without that, it's _computational process_ (unit tests, patterns, refactoring) not computational _thinking_. |
| **V failure = design process without design thinking**                               | Cargo-culting wireframes, personas, A/B tests, design-thinking steps without twinning the user. Symptoms: cannot answer specific user/audience/persona questions; cannot connect features to user characteristics/needs; cannot provide user/persona validations or evidence for design decisions.                                                                                                     |
| **F failure = computational process without computational thinking**                 | Cargo-culting unit tests, architecture/paradigm patterns, refactoring moves without an NM-model. Symptoms: only black-box not structural/glass-box tests; using patterns subtly incompatible with the underlying language/framework.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Twin-failure categories stay** (R5 lock; reconfirmed in R7)                        | Twin ignored / Twin wrong / Twin not-yet-known — apply equally to V and F. These are the _kinds_ of twin breakdown that produce the symptoms.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| **The twin / process 2×2**                                                           | Four corners: no twin = pure process; user-twin only = V (Ch3 develops this); NM-twin only = F (Ch2 develops this); both twins = the transcendent state Ch4 + Ch5 develop. Three of four corners get chapter mappings; the no-twin corner is the starting position the curriculum brings learners out of.                                                                                              |
| **Vibe coding placement**                                                            | Vibe coding (Karpathy) is NOT bound to a single corner of the 2×2. With user-twin it's LLM-collaborative Vibetoading (V corner); without user-twin it's the no-twin corner. _Which twin (if any)_ the practitioner is holding is what places it on the grid.                                                                                                                                           |
| **Twin-less processes have value, but are not a substitute for twinning**            | They evolved as effective guardrails / guides / checklists. A learner with disciplined process but no twin can _sometimes reach similar outcomes_ to a practitioner with strong twinning but loose process, especially in well-bounded settings. NOT claimed: process-without-twin outperforms twin-without-process.                                                                                   |
| **Strict process _with_ strong twinning is itself a failure mode**                   | The processes are descriptive — derived retrospectively from observing practitioners who twin well (music theory as descriptive observations of master composers, mentioned in passing). A strongly-twinning practitioner who enforces processes strictly loses the responsiveness their twin and automated experience provide. _You have to understand the rules in order to break them._             |
| **Healthy relationship between twin and process**                                    | Processes _afford_ structure; twins _do_ the work; experience automates the integration.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Documentation as a both-hats case**                                                | Technical documentation requires both dev/reader twinning (V-shape) AND NM-twinning at the documented software's level of abstraction (F-shape). Building an API/library is creating a new NM layered on top of the underlying language; documenting it well requires twinning that NM. Worth explaining for learners.                                                                                 |
| **V/F personality "Watchpoint" lines dropped**                                       | The personality cards previously had Watchpoint lines (R5 addition). Now redundant with the symptoms section. Cards just show Stance / Energy / Tools of trade / Tells / Strengths.                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Symptoms section**                                                                 | ceremony-without-twin (F side) and design-thinking-process-without-user-twin (V side) are SYMPTOMS, not failure modes themselves. The failure is upstream, in the twin (or in strict-process-enforcement when already twinning).                                                                                                                                                                       |
