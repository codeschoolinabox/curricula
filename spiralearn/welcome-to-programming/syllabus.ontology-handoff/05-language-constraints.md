## Style / voice constraints

- **Reign in wannabe-GEB.** Don't write to impress fellow intellectuals. Write to teach.
- **Light and fun.** "It's supposed to stay light and fun to keep the learner engaged" (user, R5).
- **Belgian-French / Flemish skepticism with factual directness.** No enthusiasm-overselling. (See user's `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` for full voice guide.)
- **Don't soften corrections.** When the user corrects, complete the cut. (Memory: "metaphor-trap-self-correction.")
- **Avoid AskUserQuestion forms during live prose iteration.** (Memory.)
- **Use mermaid only when relationships need it.** Otherwise tables, lists, prose, or simple ASCII.

## What to do when in doubt about a term

1. Check `02-decisions-log.md`
2. Check `03-open-threads.md` (some terms are still being decided)
3. Look at user's `syllabus.clauding-{2,3,4}.txt` files for exact phrasing
4. Default: ask the user. Don't lock vocabulary unilaterally.
