## Why this file exists

The composer/virtuoso/mechanism metaphor is rich enough to deserve its
own home. It currently lives in two places:

- `syllabus.md` (706–737) carries the introductory framing for new
  readers
- `syllabus.ontology.md` §25 carries the full reference treatment

Wave 3 consolidates both into this file as the canonical home; `syllabus.md`
keeps a gesture + reading pointer, and ontology §25 collapses to a
short reference (with the metaphor's claims about V/F lens-pair
illustration retained where they ground V/F readings).

The metaphor's principles:

- **First-class teaching apparatus.** It illuminates moments.
- **NOT structural guide.** The spiral curriculum (§6) is the
  structural guide.
- **Composer ≈ V's lens on the artifact**; **virtuoso ≈ F's lens on
  the artifact's notation-execution**. The cast is an illustration of
  the V/F lens-pair (§2) and §17's strata, not a separate ontological
  commitment.
