# Sandbox

Checkpoint pages for the study-lenses orchestrate region. Each surface lands
with its own page as its wave delivers it:

- `editor/` — the editing surface
- `phases-panel/` — the five-phase study panel
- `level-ui/` — the level selector and strict toggle
- `orchestrate/` — the full instrument
- `generator/` — the AI-authoring view, mounted alone over the placeholder
  socket; the region's own wiring of it is exercised on the `orchestrate/` page
- `cutover/` — proves which orchestrator tree `MDXComponents.js` currently
  renders through the `lenses.json` cascade, not a hardcoded harness

Pages import region components under `@docusaurus/BrowserOnly` — Docusaurus
pre-renders in node, and the editor's CodeMirror surface exists only in a
browser.
